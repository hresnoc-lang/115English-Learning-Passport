⭐ Level 1

Yes, please.

⭐ Level 2

Where is she from?
She’s from India.
Is he from Japan?
No, he isn’t. He’s from Spain.

⭐ Level 3

A big black bug bit a big black bear, made the big black bear bleed blood.
🎤 這是繞口令練習。建議先點單字熟悉發音，再慢速朗讀整句，最後逐漸加快。
